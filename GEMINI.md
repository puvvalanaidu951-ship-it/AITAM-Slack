# AITAM SLACK / College Connect Project Guidelines

Always adhere strictly to these principles for all development, design, and content tasks:

1. **Aesthetic & UI Standards:**
   - Never use purple gradients or "vibe-coded" AI aesthetics.
   - Never use pill-shaped buttons; use standard rounded rects (8-12px radius).
   - No cursor animations, distracting scroll parallax, or flashy gimmick animations.
   - No emoji icons in place of UI icons (always use clean SVG iconography).

2. **Content & Copy Integrity:**
   - No vague hero copy, AI-slop text, or stock imagery.
   - No fake reviews, testimonials, or artificial counters/metrics.
   - Do not use em dashes (`—` or `&mdash;`). Use colons, commas, or standard hyphens (`-`).

3. **Production Completeness:**
   - Ensure the favicon (`assets/aitam.png`) is linked across all pages in the header.
   - Never include "Made with AI" tags or watermarks.
   - Ensure the Privacy Policy (`privacy.php`) and Terms & Conditions (`terms.php`) pages are present and linked in the footer.
   - Code must be robust, secure, and error-free.
